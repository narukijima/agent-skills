# Platform and surface matrix

This matrix selects the right reference. It does not claim that every listed stage is public.

| Platform | Initial surfaces | Public evidence strength | Important separation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | For You Home Timeline, Following, Search Top, Explore/Trends, Conversations, Notifications, account recommendations | Deep code evidence for For You; product-level official material for other surfaces | Do not apply For You code weights to Search, replies, Following, or account recommendations |
| YouTube | Home, Suggested/Up Next, Shorts Feed, Search, Subscriptions/destination shelves | Strong official help/creator explanation; some historical papers | Search optimizes query relevance/engagement/quality; Home and Up Next use different primary context |
| Facebook | Feed connected inventory, Feed Recommendations/Home discovery, Reels, Stories, Search, Groups and other cards | Official system cards and engineering/product explanations | Connected Feed ranking and unconnected recommendations are not one inventory |
| Instagram | Feed connected content, Feed Recommendations, Stories, Explore, Reels, Search, suggested accounts | Official system cards plus Explore engineering detail | Feed, Feed Recommendations, Explore, Reels, Stories, Search remain separate surfaces |
| Threads | For You/recommended feed, Following, Search, Trending, custom/community feeds | Sparse first-party product and creator guidance; no public end-to-end ranking specification found | Do not inherit Instagram or Facebook mechanisms without Threads-specific evidence |
| TikTok | For You, Following, Friends, LIVE, Search, comments, account recommendations | Current official support gives surface-specific factor categories; older Newsroom explains For You | For You interaction weighting does not establish Search ranking; eligibility is separate from ranking |

Before analysis, map the user's metric to its distribution source. If the data aggregates multiple surfaces, either segment it or lower confidence.
